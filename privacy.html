Privacy Policy

Claude Gmail Reader · Last updated 29 September 2026

Who uses it

Claude Gmail Reader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Matthew Johan, with his own Google account. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

It requests one Google permission: gmail.readonly, which allows reading messages in the owner's Gmail. It cannot send, modify or delete email, and it does not access contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • Messages are searched only to find receipts, invoices and travel confirmations for the owner's own expense reports.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No copy of the mailbox is kept. Details taken from a receipt (merchant, date, amount) may be written into the owner's own expense files.

Credentials

The access credential is stored in the owner's private settings and is never published. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Claude Gmail Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
